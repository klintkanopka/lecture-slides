# Guest Lecture: George Perrett


- George is a first year Ph.D student in the Applied Statistics and Computational Social Science Program working at the intersection of causal inferences and machine learning.

- Prior to his doctoral studies he was awarded a Masters of Science in Applied Statistics (NYU), a Masters of Public Health (University of Michigan), and a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology (University of Wisconsin).
